Career Overview

Hardik Pandya is one of India’s premier pace-bowling all-rounders, known for explosive batting and impactful spells in limited-overs cricket.

International Career

ODI Debut: 2016 vs New Zealand
T20I Debut: 2016 vs Australia
Test Debut: 2017 vs Sri Lanka
Key member of India’s white-ball squads in ICC tournaments
Served as India’s T20I captain in multiple series

IPL Career

Made his IPL debut in 2015 for Mumbai Indians
Played a major role in multiple IPL title wins (2015, 2017, 2019, 2020)
Became captain of Gujarat Titans in 2022
Led Gujarat Titans to an IPL title in their debut season (2022)
Returned to Mumbai Indians as captain ahead of IPL 2024

91 (34) vs Bangladesh – ICC T20 World Cup 2016

A brutal counterattack at Bengaluru where Hardik Pandya smashed 91 off 34 balls to rescue India from trouble.
His late-overs assault powered India to a match-winning total in a must-win World Cup clash.

76 (43) vs Pakistan – Asia Cup 2022

Under intense pressure, Pandya played a composed yet explosive knock of 76 in Dubai.
He finished the game with a six, sealing a thrilling win over arch-rivals Pakistan.

92 (76) vs Australia – Test Debut 2017

On his Test debut against Australia in Dharamshala, Pandya scored a fearless 92.
His attacking approach shifted momentum and showcased his ability in the longest format.
